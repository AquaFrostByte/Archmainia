Archmania

Archmania is a silly little rhythm game!

There is not much to it, just click the notes and try to get the hightest scores possible!

If you are good enough, you can get featured on the Leaderboard! There are diffrent levels in the base game, although most levels should be added by the user.

But dont worry, it wont be to hard! Its made easy by justy importing Osu Maps.
